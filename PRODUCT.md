# Product

Original Android 8+ full-panel companion for compatible blackbox-fm1 firmware:
14 buttons, seven encoders, 27 keys, USB host MIDI and standard BLE-MIDI.
Audio stays on the pedal. MASTER can only lower the physical volume ceiling.
No screen/parameter mirroring, Q mini routing or firmware installer.
REC held + F3 clears a selected sequence, not the entire song order.
Development-signed APKs only for this alpha.
